The problem is strongest in the evening.

03 · It wasn’t everywhere

A small set of stores carries disproportionate impact.

A retailer-wide intervention would be less precise than targeting the locations generating the highest excess cancellations.

06 · What I would do next

Detect earlier. Diagnose deeper. Prioritize by impact.

This is where the analysis becomes an operating recommendation rather than a reporting exercise.

01

Investigate inventory synchronization

Start with the highest-impact evening stores and verify availability accuracy.

02

Validate store-hour accuracy

Early closures cluster in evening windows, creating an actionable operations hypothesis.

03

Upgrade sustained-change monitoring

Use a fixed historical baseline or change-detection method instead of relying only on a rolling threshold.

04

Measure intervention impact

Re-run cancellation, timeliness and fill-rate metrics after corrective action.
